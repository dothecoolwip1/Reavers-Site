Arrive in gym clothes

Indoor shoes, comfortable athletic clothing, water, and a protective cup if you have one. That is enough to start.

Learn movement & contact

Footwork, stance, safe falling, basic striking mechanics, clinch positions, and how the rules change what works.

Build soft kit skills

Use padded equipment to develop timing, pressure, teamwork, and confidence without the cost and weight of armour.

Progress into steel

Armoured work comes when your skills, conditioning, equipment, and team readiness line up.

02 / WHAT WE TRAIN

Train the athlete before the armour.

The strongest clubs we researched treat Buhurt as a real sport, not costume fighting. Our training path follows that same idea.

Fight fitness

Conditioning, loaded movement, grip, carries, explosive work, and recovering while tired.

Grappling

Clinch control, balance, takedowns, rail pressure, trips, and staying useful when space disappears.

Weapons

Legal striking, mechanics, targeting, control, dueling fundamentals, and using a weapon without forgetting the fight around you.

Team tactics

Communication, target selection, protecting teammates, pressure, movement, and finishing advantages together.

Armour mobility

Learning how your kit changes breathing, vision, balance, heat, movement, and decision making.

Competition prep

Rules, checks, equipment readiness, fight plans, recovery, and the practical details of tournament day.
