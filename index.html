WHAT WE DO

Fight as a team. Train like athletes.

Buhurt is a modern combat sport fought in historically inspired steel armour. The Reavers train the physical skills, technical skills, and teamwork it takes to compete.

TEAM COMBAT

Melee

Striking, grappling, takedowns, rail work, and team tactics under pressure.

ONE ON ONE

Duels

Fast technical weapon rounds scored on clean legal strikes.

FULL CONTACT

Outrance

Timed rounds combining striking, clinch work, and grappling in armour.

THE TEAM

Armour gets attention. The people make the team.

The Reavers are fighters, training partners, squires, organizers, volunteers, photographers, and friends. Competition matters, but so does building a club people actually want to stay in.

STARTING OUT

Your first night is simple.

We are not expecting a finished fighter. Come see the sport, meet the team, and learn the basics before worrying about armour.

01

Message us

We confirm practice is running and answer the obvious questions.

02

Wear gym gear

Indoor shoes, athletic clothes, water. That is enough to begin.

03

Try the basics

Movement, safe contact, soft kit, and the feel of the team before committing.
