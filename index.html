This is Buhurt

Medieval armor. Modern combat sport.

Buhurt is a full contact combat sport fought in historically inspired steel armor with blunted steel weapons. Fighters strike, grapple, clinch, trip, and work as a team inside a fenced list.

You do not need a martial arts background, your own armor, or tournament experience to start. You need curiosity, a willingness to learn, and enough patience to build the skills safely.

01

Learn first

New people start with movement, conditioning, controlled drills, footwork, and safe contact before armor becomes the focus.

02

Gear up gradually

Limited loaner gear can help you try the sport when fit permits. Nobody needs to buy a full kit just to see whether Buhurt is for them.

03

Train for the list

Practices build conditioning, striking, grappling, rail work, communication, and the decision making that matters in a live fight.

04

Compete when ready

The team attends Buhurt events in Alberta and beyond. Competition is a goal, not a requirement for belonging at practice.

Your first practice

Show up curious. We will walk you through the rest.

You are welcome to watch, ask questions, try soft kit drills, and move at a pace that makes sense. The goal of a first visit is to understand the sport, not prove anything.
