02 / WHAT WE FIGHT

More than one way to enter the list.

Buhurt is a modern combat sport fought in historically inspired armour. Different formats reward different skills, but every category demands conditioning, control, technique, and composure under pressure.

TEAM COMBAT

Buhurt melee

Team versus team. Strikes, grappling, takedowns, rail work, communication, and pressure. Last side standing takes the round.

1 VS 1

Duels

Technical weapon categories scored on clean, legal strikes. Fast decisions, clean mechanics, and repeatable skill.

1 VS 1

Outrance

Armoured combat at its most MMA-like. Striking, grappling, clinch work, and sustained pressure over timed rounds.

03 / TRAINING

Your path to steel starts without steel.

Good clubs around the world do not throw beginners straight into armour. Neither do we. You build movement, conditioning, clinch skills, safe striking, team habits, and confidence before expensive gear becomes the priority.
