WHAT WE CAN DO

More than a fight in armour.

Armoured combat gets attention, but a good Reavers appearance gives people context too. We can combine demonstrations, equipment, sport education, questions, photos, and audience interaction into something that fits the group and space.

01

LIVE DEMONSTRATION

Armoured combat

Controlled live combat with an explanation of the rules, equipment, safety, tactics, and what the audience is watching.

02

LEARN

Armour & sport education

See modern buhurt equipment up close, learn how it is used, and hear how the sport connects historical inspiration with modern competition.

03

MEET THE TEAM

Questions & photos

A relaxed option for groups that want to meet fighters, ask questions, see armour, and take photos without needing a full combat demonstration.

04

BUILD YOUR FORMAT

Mix what fits

Most appearances can combine pieces of the options above. We confirm what works after we know the venue, audience, timing, and team availability.

GOOD FIT FOR

Community looks different everywhere.

You do not need to know exactly what to ask for. Tell us what you are planning and who will be there. We can work out the right format with you.

SchoolsClass visits, history connections, sport education, activity days.
Homeschool groupsInteractive learning sessions, armour, questions, and demonstrations.
Festivals & fairsPublic demonstrations, scheduled appearances, and crowd friendly explanations.
Markets & community daysMeet the team, armour displays, recruitment, photos, and short demos where suitable.
Birthdays & private groupsA memorable introduction to real armoured combat built around the age group and space.
Local partnersSponsorship, event partnerships, venues, fundraising, and community collaboration.

HOW IT WORKS

Start simple.

Submitting an inquiry does not lock either side into a booking. It gives us enough information to check the team, venue, safety needs, travel, and the format that makes sense.

1

Tell us about it

Share the date if you have one, location, audience, and the kind of event or group.

2

We shape the idea

We look at team availability and what type of appearance fits the space and audience.

3

Confirm the details

We agree on timing, setup, safety boundaries, travel, expectations, and any costs or donations before the event.

BOOK / PARTNER / ASK

Tell us what you are thinking.

You do not need a finished plan. Send the basics and the Reavers can figure out whether we are a fit.
