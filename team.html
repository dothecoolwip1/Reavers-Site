TEAM ROLES

There is more than one way to be a Reaver.

Buhurt needs people inside the armour and people around it. A strong team makes both visible.

IN THE LIST

Fighters

Train for melee, duels, outrance, demonstrations, and competition.

ON THE RAIL

Squires

Armour, hydration, repairs, timing, and helping fighters stay functional through tournament day.

TEAM OPS

COMMUNITY

TEAM CULTURE

What we expect from each other.

The club should be competitive without becoming miserable to be around. That means standards in training and standards in how people treat each other.

01

Team before ego

Melee rewards people who do the job the team needs.

02

Progress honestly

Skills, conditioning, contact, armour, and competition all come in the right order.

03

Keep showing up

Consistency matters more than looking impressive for one practice.
