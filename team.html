THE PEOPLE INSIDE THE ARMOUR

TEAM

Competitive when it is time to compete. Welcoming when somebody is new. Built around people who keep showing up for each other.

WHO WE ARE

A team first.

The armour is loud. The club behind it matters more. We want people to have a clear place to learn, contribute, compete, and stick around.

01

Team over ego

Melee works when everyone does the job the team needs, not when everyone tries to be the hero.

02

Earn progression

Skills, fitness, contact, soft kit, armour, competition. We build in layers instead of rushing the expensive part.

03

Show up

Consistency builds fighters and teams. Training matters even when there is no tournament next weekend.

04

Grow the sport

Demos, newcomers, community events, and helping other clubs matter to the future of Buhurt in Western Canada.
