THE PEOPLE INSIDE THE ARMOUR

THE TEAM

The Reavers are fighters, training partners, organizers, squires, volunteers, friends, and the people who make sure everyone gets home after a hard day in steel.

01 / WHO WE ARE

Built together.

We want the Red Deer Reavers to feel like a real sports team: competitive when it is time to compete, welcoming when somebody is new, and organized enough that people can grow into the sport without figuring everything out alone.

01

Team over ego

Melee is not won by five people trying to be the hero. We train communication, trust, and doing the job the team needs.

02

Earn progression

We build skills in layers. Fitness, safe contact, soft kit, armour, and competition all come when they make sense.

03

Show up

Consistency matters. The strongest teams in this sport are built by people who train even when there is no tournament next weekend.

04

Grow the sport

Demos, newcomers, community events, and helping other clubs all matter if Buhurt is going to keep getting better in Western Canada.

02 / THERE IS A ROLE

You do not need to fight to be a Reaver.

Great armored combat teams need more than fighters. We want clear ways for people to contribute whether they are competing, learning, supporting events, helping with armour, taking media, or just becoming part of the crew.

IN THE LIST

Fighters

Train for melee, duels, outrance, and the physical demands of competing in armour.

ON THE RAIL

Squires & support

Help fighters armour up, manage equipment, hydration, repairs, timing, and the chaos around tournament day.

BEHIND THE SCENES

Team crew

Photography, video, fundraising, event support, organization, demonstrations, and community outreach all help the team move.

03 / IN THE WILD

This is what the team actually looks like.

No stock knights. No fantasy renders. The site uses the Reavers' own training and team photography because the best way to understand the club is to see the people already doing it.
